# coordexp-research-memory Specification

## Purpose
Make CoordExp research context understandable as a source-linked line of inquiry, preserving global milestones, local decisions, scientific limitations and current-owner authority.
## Requirements
### Requirement: Configured research narrative
CoordExp context SHALL organize the long-term objective, route-wide milestones, relevant branch and turning points, accepted outcome, limits and remaining discriminating questions. Project-specific interpretation SHALL be configured over existing research owners rather than hardcoded as generic memory behavior.

#### Scenario: Airport branch is the current topic
- **WHEN** an Agent requests the relevant research context
- **THEN** the summary places finite-fit and candidate-recovery milestones between the long-term objective and the airport branch, then explains why entrance repair led to a successor-composition question

### Requirement: Preserve scientific object levels
The research lens MUST distinguish physical objects from annotation matches, supplied/forced behavior from natural generation, finite-panel fit from transfer, focal gains from source-object losses, and technical completion from scientific support. Summaries SHALL retain decision-changing negative results and limitations.

#### Scenario: Local composition succeeds but preservation fails
- **WHEN** a candidate naturally reports the background aircraft and worker but loses clear guard-image objects
- **THEN** context reports both the local success and failed joint-preservation criterion without presenting the candidate as a clean repair or promoted model

#### Scenario: Spatial cue succeeds on one training image
- **WHEN** nine additional visually supported bottles are summarized alongside a transfer case with no confirmed addition
- **THEN** the supplied-cue, training-case and finite-transfer conditions remain explicit, without claiming autonomous population-level recall improvement

### Requirement: Scoped current state and latest decisions
The remembered narrative SHALL retain its as-of date and source pointers. Before a current research judgment, the foreground Agent SHALL inspect the relevant current index/question/unit/result owners and applicable later explicit user decisions. Normal source edits MUST NOT erase the dated research account. Closure MUST be scoped to the named branch. Proposed preparation, executed work, accepted evidence and promotion SHALL remain distinct, and historical grants MUST NOT authorize a new run.

#### Scenario: Closed airport sequence and new preparation coexist
- **WHEN** the sparse-token airport sequence is closed while a separate multi-scene investigation is preparing
- **THEN** the summary preserves both states rather than calling the entire project stopped or treating the new design as an experimental result

### Requirement: Original owners retain research authority
Memory SHALL retain usable pointers to formal research and evidence owners. A Dreaming-derived account SHALL remain a summary; suggested improvements to those owners SHALL remain topic-linked proposals until a later Agent or the user decides under current authority. A missing explanation SHALL remain unknown or labeled inference rather than a fabricated historical motive.

#### Scenario: Conversation contains a turning point absent from the question page
- **WHEN** Dreaming finds sourced material that could clarify the formal research narrative
- **THEN** it records the current decision with its provenance and leaves an owner-update proposal rather than silently rewriting the formal page

### Requirement: Bounded qualification claims
Acceptance SHALL separately report publication, explicit reading, automatic final-context delivery, source/review coverage and model use or benefit. The user-deferred model-quality study MUST NOT be treated as completed by deterministic checks, and a bounded content pilot MUST NOT count as full historical bootstrap.

#### Scenario: Existing gateway can read the approved synopsis explicitly
- **WHEN** a 3,915-character synopsis is successfully read with a declared adequate budget but default consumption omits it
- **THEN** the content/explicit-read trial passes only its stated boundary and default integration remains an implementation task
